Help & Support
Guides and resources for using SAFEWARE
Importing Inventory
Go to Inventory and click New Batch. Upload a CSV or Excel file — SAFEWARE automatically maps columns, matches chemicals by CAS number or name, and flags anything that needs review before import.
Warehouse Layout
Open Warehouse to place chemicals into sections. Use Auto-Arrange for AI-powered safe placement based on reactivity rules. Incompatible chemicals are automatically separated — red borders indicate unsafe pairs.
Compatibility Analysis
Select chemicals in your inventory and run Analyze to get a full compatibility matrix. Results are color-coded: green = compatible, amber = caution, red = incompatible. Export to Excel for compliance reporting.
EU Compliance Export
Go to Compliance to generate an enterprise Excel report with EU regulatory data (SVHC, Annex XIV, Annex XVII restrictions) cross-referenced against your inventory. Sheet-protected, audit-ready format.
